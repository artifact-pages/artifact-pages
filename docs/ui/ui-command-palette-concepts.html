Matched characters are highlighted in place. Title and path stay readable as one compact result.

How the hit is explained

Follow the highlighted letters in the title and path. The field is clear from the row’s natural hierarchy.

Best when

Search should feel immediate, with almost no extra chrome around the result.

Trade-off: in a long or ambiguous result list, the reader has to inspect the highlights rather than scan a field label.
